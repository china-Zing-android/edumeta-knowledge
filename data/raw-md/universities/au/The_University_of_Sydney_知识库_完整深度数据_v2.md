### 2.4 English Language Waiver

Applicants who have completed **prior study in English** (minimum 1–2 years FT) in an institution where English is the medium of instruction in an English-speaking country (Australia, UK, USA, Canada, New Zealand, Ireland, South Africa) may have the English requirement waived. The university also accepts:

- **Centre for English Teaching (CET)**: The University of Sydney's English language centre offers Direct Entry Course (DEC) pathways — completion at the required level meets English requirements for most programs.
- **USYD Foundation Program**: From Taylors College/University of Sydney Foundation Program can also satisfy English requirements.

| Intake | Start Date | Application Deadline (International) | Notes |
|--------|-----------|--------------------------------------|-------|

> **Note**: Deadlines may vary by program. Some competitive programs (Medicine, Dentistry, Law, Physiotherapy) have **earlier** deadlines and may close applications once quotas are filled.

### 3.2 Application Important Dates

| Milestone | Date (Semester 1, 2027) |
|-----------|-------------------------|
| Applications open | **Ongoing** |
| Early entry applications | Varies by program |
| Offer rounds | **Rolling** (usually within 2–4 weeks of application) |
| Deadline to accept offer | Usually **4–6 weeks** from date of offer |
| Deadline to pay first tuition fee | Varies; typically 2–4 weeks after acceptance |
| OSHC commencement | Must cover from visa grant date |
| Visa application deadline | Recommend 6–8 weeks before semester start |

### 3.3 Application Process Timeline

1. **Prepare documents**: Academic transcripts, English test results, personal statement (if required), CV (if required), portfolio (for creative programs), references (for research programs)
2. **Apply online**: Via the University of Sydney Online Application Portal (UAC for domestic; International Application for international)
3. **Assessment**: University assesses application (2–4 weeks typical; longer for competitive programs)
4. **Offer**: Unconditional or Conditional Letter of Offer
5. **Acceptance**: Accept offer + pay deposit / first semester tuition fee + OSHC
6. **Confirmation of Enrolment (CoE)**: Issued after acceptance/fee payment
7. **Student Visa**: Apply for Australian student visa (subclass 500) with CoE
8. **Pre-departure**: Organise accommodation, travel, arrival services

### 3.4 Late Applications

The University of Sydney typically accepts late applications for most programs on a rolling basis, subject to program availability. However, competitive programs (Medicine, high-demand programs) may close applications once filled.

**Source**: https://www.sydney.edu.au/study/how-to-apply/international-students/application-dates.html
