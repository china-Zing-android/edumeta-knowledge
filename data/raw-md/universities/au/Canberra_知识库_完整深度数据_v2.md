## Section 3 — Application Requirements & Deadlines

### 3.1 Entry Requirements — Undergraduate (Domestic)

| 入学方式 | 要求 |
|----------|------|
| **Standard entry (ATAR)** | Minimum ATAR 60 (大多数课程). 部分竞争性课程要求更高 (如 Physiotherapy, Pharmacy) |
| **School leaver** | Year 12 completion with ATAR; 可通过 Adjustment Factors 加分 |
| **TAFE/CIT qualification** | Diploma or Advanced Diploma from accredited VET provider; 可获学分减免 |
| **Previous university study** | Completed tertiary studies; 可申请学分转换 |
| **Work experience** | Professional experience considered for mature-age applicants via alternative entry |
| **Bridging/enabling programs** | UC College diplomas, UC Connect, UC Preparation Scheme → guaranteed entry pathway |
| **Special entry** | Australian Indigenous students (Ngunnawal Centre support), equity access schemes |

### 3.2 English Language Requirements

| Test | Undergraduate Minimum | Postgraduate Minimum |
|------|---------------------|---------------------|
| IELTS (Academic) | Overall 6.0, no band < 6.0 (大多数课程) | Overall 6.5-7.0 (因课程而异) |
| TOEFL iBT | 60+ (varies by course) | 79+ (varies by course) |
| PTE Academic | 50+ (varies by course) | 58+ (varies by course) |

> **Note**: Some professional programs (Nursing, Pharmacy, Physiotherapy, Teaching) have higher English requirements. Check individual course pages.

### 3.3 Application Deadlines

| Intake | Application Opens | Application Deadline | Semester Start |
|--------|------------------|---------------------|----------------|
| **Semester 2 2026** | Open now | Rolling admissions | 10 August 2026 |
| **Semester 1 2027** | Open | Rolling admissions | 15 February 2027 |

> UC uses rolling admissions — apply early for competitive programs.
> International students should apply at least 8-12 weeks before intake.

### 3.4 Application Process

- **Domestic UG**: Apply via QTAC (Queensland Tertiary Admissions Centre)
- **Domestic PG**: Direct application to UC
- **International**: Direct application to UC via international application portal
- **Research degrees**: Direct application with research proposal and supervisor confirmation

## Section 4 — Costs & Financial Aid

### 4.1 Tuition Fees

**Domestic students (Commonwealth Supported Place — CSP):**
- Most undergraduate courses are CSP supported
- Student contribution amounts vary by discipline band:
  - Band 1 (Humanities): ~$4,000–$8,000/year (estimated)
  - Band 2 (Science/Engineering): ~$8,000–$15,000/year (estimated)
  - Band 3 (Law/Business/Medicine): ~$11,000–$16,000/year (estimated)
- HECS-HELP loan available for eligible domestic students (defer payment via tax system)

**International students:**
- Fees vary by course (AUD/year, approximate ranges):
  - Arts/Humanities: $28,000–$32,000
  - Business/Law: $30,000–$36,000
  - Science/IT: $32,000–$38,000
  - Health (Nursing/Pharmacy): $34,000–$40,000
  - Professional Health (Physiotherapy/Occupational Therapy): $40,000–$46,000

> **Note**: Per Australian university pattern, specific fee amounts are listed on each individual course's "Fees & scholarships" page. The central fees page explains fee types only. See sample course URLs for per-program fees.

### 4.2 Additional Costs

| Cost Item | Amount (per year) |
|-----------|-------------------|
| Student Services & Amenities Fee (SSAF) | ~$300–$350/year |
| Textbooks & study materials | ~$500–$1,500/year |
| Accommodation (on campus) | ~$200–$350/week |
| Living expenses | ~$20,000–$25,000/year (estimated) |

### 4.3 Scholarships

UC offers hundreds of scholarships for domestic and international students across UG, PG, and research levels. Key scholarship types:
- **Academic merit scholarships** (high ATAR achievers)
- **Equity scholarships** (financial hardship, regional/remote)
- **Indigenous scholarships** (Australian Indigenous students)
- **International student scholarships** (partial tuition fee waivers)
- **Research scholarships** (RTP, UC HDR stipends)

### 6.2 Data Quality Notes

- **Program classification**: Faculty attribution was inferred from course code prefixes and program names. For programs spanning multiple faculties (e.g., double degrees), the primary faculty was used.
- **Course URLs**: Each program has a canonical URL in the format `/course/{code}`. Additionally, marketing landing pages exist at `/future-students/study-at-uc/about-our-courses/{name}` for select programs.
- **Fee data**: Specific fee amounts are per-course and not available on central fee pages. The range estimates come from industry knowledge and will need per-course verification.
- **School-level hierarchy**: UC does not formally list schools/departments on faculty pages. The hierarchy in section 0.2 is reconstructed from discipline groupings on course pages.
