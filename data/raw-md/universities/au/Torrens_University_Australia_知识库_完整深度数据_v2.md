## Section 3 — Application requirements & deadlines

### Application process

- **Open entry**: No application fee for either domestic or international students applying directly to Torrens University
- **Apply anytime**: Applications accepted year-round for the next available intake
- **How to apply online**: https://apply.torrens.edu.au/
- **Alternative pathways**: Available for students without standard entry qualifications (no ATAR entry pathways, mature-age entry, work/life experience assessment)

### Application deadlines & key dates

Torrens University operates on a **trimester system** with **4 main intakes per year** plus **2 accelerated intakes**.

**Notes**:
- Accelerated intakes apply to intensive subjects only, for select courses and campuses
- Main Intake 4 offers select courses only
- Blue Mountains International Hotel Management School has different dates
- MBA (On Demand) has 10 intakes per year (Flex model)
- Bachelor of Nursing has different dates (no Main Intake 2; Main Intake 3 for Diploma of Nursing holders with AHPRA registration)
- Ducere-partnered courses and Flight Centre courses have different dates

### English language requirements

> **Note**: Per-program English language requirements (IELTS/TOEFL/PTE) were not available from the overview pages. These are specified per course on individual course detail pages. Students should check course-specific entry requirements.

### Entry pathways

- **High school students**: Year 12 completion or equivalent; Early Entry Program available for conditional offers
- **Mature-age students**: For those who completed Year 12 more than 2 years ago, have VET qualifications, or work/life experience
- **International students**: Apply through the international admissions process; student visa required
- **No ATAR entry pathways**: Available for students who didn't achieve the desired ATAR
- **Portfolios and interviews**: Required for some courses (e.g., Design)
- **Credit transfer**: Internal and external credit transfer available
- **Transfers**: Students transferring from other universities welcome

## Section 4 — Costs & financial aid

### Tuition fees

> **Note**: Torrens University provides fee schedules via downloadable PDFs (Domestic Fees PDF and International Fees PDF) rather than on-page tables. The PDF URLs are JavaScript-triggered downloads and could not be directly extracted. Fees vary by course, study level, and campus.

### Key fee information

- **FEE-HELP**: Available for eligible domestic students at diploma level and above
- **HELP Loan Limit (2026)**: $129,883 (standard), $186,544 (approved medicine/dentistry/veterinary science courses)
- **No FEE-HELP loan fee**: Torrens University is a Table B provider, so the 20% loan fee on undergraduate study does not apply (since 1 Jan 2019)
- **Repayment threshold (2025-2026)**: $67,000 income
- **VET Student Loan**: Available for CHC51015 Diploma of Counselling
- **No application fee**: No administration or application fees for direct applications

### Scholarships

Torrens University offers a wide range of scholarships:
- **International Scholarships**: For new international students
- **Domestic Scholarships**: For Australian domestic students
- **Indigenous Student Scholarships**: For Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander students
- **Alumni Scholarships**: For Torrens graduates pursuing further study
- **Industry Scholarships**: For professionals upgrading qualifications
- **Refer a Friend Scholarship**: For nominating a friend to study
