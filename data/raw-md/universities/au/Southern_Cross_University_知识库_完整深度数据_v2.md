## Section 3 — Application Requirements & Deadlines

### 3.1 English Language Requirements (International Students)

SCU uses IELTS band scores as the primary benchmark. Comparable scores for TOEFL iBT, PTE, Cambridge (CPE/CAE), ISPLR, and CEFR are accepted.

| IELTS Band | Comparable TOEFL iBT | PTE | 
|-----------|---------------------|-----|
| 4.5 (no band < 4.0) | 32–34 | 30 |
