## Section 3 — 本科招生数据 (Undergraduate Admissions)

### 3.1 基本入学要求 (Basic Entry Requirements)

**Academic:**
- Australian Year 12 (ATAR): Minimum varies by course (typically 70-99+; Bachelor of Engineering minimum ATAR 80; Bachelor of Commerce range ~75-90)
- International Baccalaureate (IB): Accepted, minimum scores vary
- Foundation programs: Go8 Foundation programs accepted
- Overseas qualifications: Assessed on a case-by-case basis

**English Language:**
- Standard minimum: IELTS 6.5 (no band below 6.0) or equivalent
- Some courses (Education, Law, Nursing, Psychology) have Higher Standard: IELTS 7.0-7.5

**Prerequisites:**
- Many courses have specific prerequisite subjects (e.g., Mathematics for Engineering, Chemistry for Medicine)
- Listed on individual course pages

**Alternative Pathways:**
- Access UWA (short courses enabling university entry)
- STAT (Skills for Tertiary Admissions Test)
- Completion of an Australian University Enabling Program
- UWA CELT (Centre for English Language Teaching) bridging programs

### 3.4 本科申请流程 (Undergraduate Application Process — International)

1. **Choose a course** — check admission requirements, prerequisites, and any additional requirements (interviews, tests)
2. **Check entry requirements** — English language + academic prerequisites
3. **Prepare supporting documents** — academic results, English test results, passport copy
4. **Apply online** — via UWA Application Portal or authorised agent
5. **Pay application fee** — AUD $150 (non-refundable)
6. **Receive outcome** — offer letter or conditional offer via email
7. **Accept offer** — through UWA Apply portal, submit required documents, make first payment
8. **Arrange visa and travel** — student visa (Subclass 500)

**Application deadlines:**

| Applicant Type | S1 2026 | S2 2026 | S1 2027 |
|---------------|---------|---------|---------|
| Domestic & Onshore | 20 Feb 2026 | 17 Jul 2026 | 19 Feb 2027 |
| All other countries | 19 Jan 2026 | 8 Jun 2026 | 11 Jan 2027 |

**Source**: https://www.uwa.edu.au/study/how-to-apply/international-applicants, https://www.uwa.edu.au/study/how-to-apply/pathways-and-eligibility/english-language-requirements

## Section 4 — 研究生招生数据 (Postgraduate Admissions)

### 4.1 研究生授课型项目 (Coursework)

**Entry Requirements:**
- Bachelor's degree from a recognised institution
- Weighted Average Mark (WAM) typically minimum 60 (varies by program)
- Some programs require relevant undergraduate major
- English language: IELTS 6.5 (no band below 6.0) standard; higher for some programs
- Additional requirements: portfolio (Design, Architecture), interview (Clinical Psychology), work experience (MBA)

**Application Process:**
- Direct application via UWA Application Portal
- Supporting documents: academic transcripts, English test results, CV, statement of purpose (where required)
- Application fee: AUD $150 (international)
- Assessment time: varies by program

### 4.2 研究生研究型项目 (Higher Degree by Research — PhD/MPhil)

**Entry Requirements:**
- Bachelor's degree with Honours (Class IIA or higher) or Master's degree with research component
- Research proposal aligned with UWA supervisor expertise
- English language: IELTS 7.0 (no band below 6.5) or equivalent (Graduate Research School standard)
- Interview may be required

**Scholarships:**
- Research Training Program (RTP): Fees offset + stipend (max 4 years PhD / 2 years MPhil)
- UWA International Research Scholarship
- University Postgraduate Award
- Industry-sponsored PhD scholarships
- Joint PhD scholarships with partner universities

**Application Process:**
1. Identify potential supervisor and develop research proposal
2. Submit via Graduate Research School application portal
3. Applications accepted year-round (some scholarship rounds have deadlines)
4. Assessment: 4-8 weeks typically

### 4.3 重要招生联系方式

- Domestic student enquiries: 131 UWA (131 892), +61 8 6488 1000
- Future Students Centre: Open 8:30am–5:00pm (AWST), Mon–Fri
- Online enquiry: AskUWA portal
- International student representatives: https://www.uwa.edu.au/study/how-to-apply/find-a-representative

**Source**: https://www.uwa.edu.au/study/how-to-apply/postgraduate, https://www.uwa.edu.au/study/how-to-apply/higher-degree-by-research

## Appendix: 技术说明与技术挑战

### 平台架构

UWA 使用 **Sitecore CMS + Next.js (React SSR) + Funnelback 搜索** 的三层架构：
- Content is managed in Sitecore and rendered via Next.js server-side rendering
- Dynamic content (fee tables, scholarship lists, English requirements accordion) is loaded client-side after React hydration
- Course search uses Funnelback proxied through Next.js — raw Funnelback API at search.uwa.edu.au returns HTTP 403 from non-browser clients
- Sitemaps contain ZERO course URLs (unlike many other AU universities)

### 数据提取限制

1. **Fee data requires interactive Fee Calculator** — no static fee pages exist; exact per-course fees must be queried via https://www.fees.uwa.edu.au/Calculator (form-based, no bulk export)
2. **Full course list requires browser automation** — 425 courses spread across 43 search pages (10/page); course slugs are not guessable from course names
3. **Client-side hydration needed** — pages like Scholarships, English Requirements, and Fees show "Loading component..." in raw HTML but render content via Next.js client-side React
4. **Dynamic content pages** — International Student Fees, Domestic Student Fees, Pathways page content is loaded via JS, not present in server-rendered HTML
