### 3.1 Entry Requirements

**Undergraduate (Domestic)**
- **ATAR-based admission**: Selection ranks (SR) displayed per degree on the degrees page (e.g., Bachelor of Arts: 63.00, Bachelor of Laws (Honours): 87.00)
- **Year 12 subject prerequisites**: Vary by program
- **Early Entry Programs**: Available for school leavers
- **Pathway Programs**: Diploma programs available (entry rank ~50.00)
- **Special Entry Schemes**: For Indigenous students, elite athletes, equity considerations, ADF experience, etc.

**Undergraduate (International)**
- Equivalent academic qualifications from home country
- Minimum academic entry requirements vary by country
- Specific subject prerequisites for some programs (e.g., Mathematics for Engineering, Science for Medicine)

**Postgraduate (Taught)**
- Bachelor's degree in relevant discipline (or equivalent)
- GPA requirements vary by program (typically equivalent of Australian Bachelors degree with minimum credit average)
- Some programs require relevant work experience (e.g., MBA)
- Specific programs have additional requirements (portfolio for Architecture, interview for Clinical Psychology)

**Postgraduate (Research)**
- Bachelor's degree with Honours (Class IIA or higher) or Master's degree with research component
- Research proposal required
