## Section 3 — Application requirements & deadlines

### 3.1 Undergraduate Entry Requirements

**General Academic Requirements**

Notre Dame uses a holistic admissions model known as "Individual Factors" — considering not just academic results but personal qualities, achievements, and community contribution.

| Pathway | Minimum Requirement |
|---------|-------------------|
| **Recent Secondary Education (ATAR)** | Indicative ATAR/Selection Rank of 70 (or IB 25) for most programs. ATAR + Adjustment Factors = Selection Rank |
| **Higher Education** | Successful completion of minimum 6 months study with at least 2 passed subjects |
| **VET Study** | Minimum Certificate IV or Diploma for most degrees |
| **Work & Life Experience** | Minimum 2 years relevant work experience |
| **STAT** | Special Tertiary Admissions Test — both written English and multiple-choice sections required |
| **Minimum Age** | 17 years of age or completed Year 12 |

**Note**: Entry into specific degrees depends on demand and available places.

**Individual Factors (Holistic Assessment)**

Applicants are asked three questions:
1. Why are you applying to study at Notre Dame?
2. Why have you chosen to study this degree?
3. Tell us how you contribute to community life
