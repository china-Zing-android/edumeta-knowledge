## Section 3 — Application Requirements & Deadlines

### 3.1 Entry Requirements

#### Domestic Students (UG)
- **ATAR-based admission**: Achieve the required ATAR for each course (varies by program, check via UAC)
- **HSC True Reward**: Early offer program based on Year 11 results
- **School Recommendation Scheme**: Early offer based on school recommendation
- **Bonus Points**: Available for eligible students (e.g., regional, subject-based)
- **The College Pathways**: Diploma and Foundation Studies for students not meeting direct entry

#### International Students
- Academic qualifications equivalent to Australian Year 12 (UG) or Bachelor's degree (PG)
- Specific country qualification requirements available in the "Entry Qualifications Table"
- Some programs have higher entry requirements or require work experience

### 3.2 English Language Requirements

#### Standard Requirements (most UG and PG programs)

| Test | Minimum Score |
|------|--------------|

| TOEFL iBT | Total 82 (Writing 21, Speaking 18, Reading 13, Listening 13) |
| PTE Academic | 58 overall, no communication skill less than 50 |

### 3.3 Application Deadlines

#### Domestic Students
- **Main Round (UAC)**: September – February (for March intake)
- **Direct Application (Western Portal)**: Rolling applications
- **Mid-Year Intake**: Applications open for July intake

#### International Students
- **March (Autumn) Intake**: Apply by December – February
- **July (Spring) Intake**: Apply by April – June
- **Rolling admissions** through International Application Portal
- Some programs have specific closing dates

### 3.4 How to Apply

- **Domestic UG**: Via UAC (application fee applies) or direct via Western Portal (free)
- **Domestic PG**: Direct via Western Portal
- **International**: Via International Application Portal (student-westernsydney.studylink.com) or through authorized education agents
- **Research**: Contact supervisor first, then apply through the HDR admissions process
