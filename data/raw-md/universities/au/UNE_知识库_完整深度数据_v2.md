## Section 7 — 跨校对比框架 (Cross-school Comparison)

UNE is a regional university based in Armidale, NSW, specialising in:
- **Online/distance education** (pioneer since 1950s, Diamond-rated digital learning)
- **Agriculture and rural science** (SMART Farms, Animal Genetics and Breeding Unit)
- **Regional health** (Joint Medical Program with University of Newcastle, rural medicine)
- **Education and teaching** (comprehensive initial teacher education programs)
- **Online flexibility** — trimester system (3 study periods/year), part-time options

**Key differentiators:**
- #1 in NSW for Social Equity (Good Universities Guide 2026)
- Only Australian public uni with 20 consecutive years of 5-star Overall Experience
- 5-star Graduate Full-Time Employment and Starting Salary
- Member of Regional Universities Network

**Campus locations:**
- Armidale (main campus, regional NSW)
- Sydney (UNE Sydney, Parramatta)
- Tamworth (UNE Tamworth)
- Taree (UNE Taree)
