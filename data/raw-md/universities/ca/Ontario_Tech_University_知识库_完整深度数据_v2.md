## Section 3 — Application requirements & deadlines (申请要求与截止日期)

### 3.1 Undergraduate Admission Requirements

#### Ontario High School Students (安大略省高中生)
- Ontario Secondary School Diploma (OSSD)
- 6 Grade 12 U or M courses, including specific program prerequisites
- Minimum overall average varies by program (competitive)
- ENG4U (English) required for most programs
- Program-specific prerequisite courses required (e.g., Advanced Functions + Calculus for Engineering)

#### Canadian High School Students (outside Ontario)
- Equivalent secondary school diploma
- Program-specific subject prerequisites
- Competitive admission based on grades

#### International Students
- Secondary school diploma equivalent to OSSD
- Country-specific academic requirements (参考国际招生页面: https://admissions.ontariotechu.ca/applicant-information/international-applicants.php)
- All bachelor degree programs are PGWP eligible
- Competitive admission — meeting minimums does not guarantee acceptance

### 3.2 Graduate Admission Requirements

- Four-year honours undergraduate degree (or equivalent)
- Minimum B average (70-75% or GPA 3.0 on 4.33 scale) in the last two years of study
- Program-specific prerequisite courses
- Letters of recommendation (typically 2-3)
- Statement of academic intent
- Transcripts and degree parchments

### 3.3 English Language Proficiency (英语语言能力要求)

#### Undergraduate ELP Requirements
以下任一条件满足：
1. First language is English
2. Completed at least 3 years of formal full-time study (minimum 3 courses/semester) at a secondary or post-secondary institution where language of instruction is English (excluding ESL programs)
3. Achieve required proficiency on acceptable English language test

Source: https://admissions.ontariotechu.ca/applicant-information/international-applicants.php (English Language Proficiency tab)

#### Graduate ELP Requirements
All applicants must provide evidence of oral and written proficiency in English. Priority given to those who meet all requirements at time of application.

Source: https://gradstudies.ontariotechu.ca/future_students/international_applicants/index.php

### 3.4 Application Deadlines

#### Undergraduate (via OUAC)
- Ontario high school students: Early admission (November), Equal consideration (January)
- International students: Apply early for study permit processing
