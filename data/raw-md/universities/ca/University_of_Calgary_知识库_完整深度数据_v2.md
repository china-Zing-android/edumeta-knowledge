## Section 1 — Undergraduate Education

### 1.1 Admission Requirements (Undergraduate)

**Application Deadlines:**
- International students: Application opens **Aug. 15** (for Fall 2026 entry)
- Domestic (Canadian) students: Application opens **Oct. 1**
- Two program choices can be selected on the application

**General Requirements:**
- High school diploma with specific course prerequisites for each program
- Competitive admission averages vary by program
- Most offers made after receiving first-term Grade 12 transcripts
- English language proficiency required for non-native speakers

**Additional Pathways to Admission:**
- **Student Access Process**: Considers factors outside academics that negatively impacted opportunity or access to university
- **Indigenous Pathways**: Automatically prompted when applicant self-identifies as Indigenous
- **University Entrance Program (UEP)**: For students not initially admissible to their desired degree program

**Transfer Credit:**
- Previous studies from other institutions can be applied toward UCalgary degree programs

### 1.2 English Language Requirement

International applicants from non-English speaking countries must demonstrate English language proficiency through one of:
- IELTS, TOEFL, CAEL, PTE Academic, or Duolingo English Test
- Minimum scores vary by program

### 3.2 Scholarships & Financial Aid

**Entrance Awards (Undergraduate):**
- $22M+ in scholarships, awards, and bursaries annually
- 35% of incoming students receive an award in first year
- **Scholarship Categories:**
  - Automatic Admission Awards (e.g., President's Admission Scholarship: $5,000 for 95%+ admission average)
  - Prestige Awards ($20,000–$100,000 value, many renewable)
  - High School Awards (1,100+ awards, $500–$10,000)

| Award Type | Deadline | Application Required | Notification |
|------------|----------|---------------------|--------------|
| Automatic Admission Awards | None | No | At time of offer |
| High School Awards & Bursaries | March 1 | Yes (one application) | May 1 |
| Prestige Awards | December 1 | Yes (essay questions) | March 1–May 1 |

**Graduate Funding:**
- Graduate Awards Database
- Research assistantships and teaching assistantships
- External scholarships (NSERC, SSHRC, CIHR, etc.)

**Payment Plans:**
- Self-enrol in payment plan for instalment payments per term

**Other Funding Sources:**
- RESP
- Student loans (Canadian, International, US)
- Part-time jobs (on-campus)
- PURE Program (Program for Undergraduate Research Experience) — up to $7,500 for summer research

### 3.3 External Awards Resources
