### 1.1 本科招生 (Undergraduate Admissions)

#### 1.1.1 招生路径 (Admission Routes)

| Route | Description |

| Inclusive Education | For students with disabilities or learning challenges |
| Indigenous Student Success Certificate (ISSC) | For Indigenous students |
| International / ELP | For international applicants; must meet English Language Proficiency |

#### 1.1.2 基本入学要求 (General Admission Requirements)

**Canadian High School Applicants:**
- 5 distinct Grade 12-level courses, including one English Language Arts
- Minimum final admission average of 65%
- Some programs have higher/additional requirements
- All applicants must meet English Language Proficiency requirement

**Post-Secondary Transfer:**
- 6+ transferable university-level courses
- Course outlines (syllabi) required for transfer credit assessment
- Three- and four-year degree holders may be considered for second degree

**Mature Student:**
- For applicants who have been out of formal education for a significant period

#### 1.1.3 英语语言要求 (English Language Proficiency - ELP)

ELP test scores must be completed within two years prior to application (effective Fall 2019).

Accepted tests (standardized English tests):
- TOEFL
- IELTS
- PTE (Pearson Test of English)
- CAEL (Canadian Academic English Language Assessment)
- CAE (Cambridge English: Advanced)
- CPE (Cambridge English: Proficiency)
- MELAB (Michigan English Language Assessment Battery)
- University of Lethbridge English for Academic Purposes (EAP) Program completion (Advanced Level)

**EAP Program** — Successful completion of Advanced Level meets ELP requirement without standardized test score.
