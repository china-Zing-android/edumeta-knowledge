## Section 2 — 录取要求 (Admission Requirements)

### 2.1 Canadian / Domestic Students

Admission requirements vary by province. General high school diploma with specific course prerequisites depending on program.

**Nova Scotia residents**: High school diploma with appropriate academic courses. Minimum averages vary by program.

Key details (from Academic Calendar / Admissions page):
- Application deadline for Fall Term (Domestic): August 1
- Application deadline for Winter Term (Domestic): December 1
- Application deadline for Spring Term (Domestic): March 1
- Application deadline for Summer Term (Domestic): May 1
- Admissions for 2026-2027 open: October 1, 2025

### 2.2 International Students

International applicants must provide:
1. Provincial Attestation Letter (PAL)
2. Study visa for Canada
3. Admission Confirmation Deposit

**Academic requirements by country** — examples (from International Admissions page):

| Country | Requirement |
|---------|-------------|
| Afghanistan | Baccalauria, Min 7/10 avg, no mark below 6/10 |
| Albania | Maturity Certificate, Min 70% avg, no mark below 60% |
| Algeria | Baccalauréat, Min 10/20 avg, no mark below 8/20 |
| Angola | Ensino Geral Certificado, Min 10/20 avg |
| Argentina | Bachillerato, Min 7/10 avg, no mark below 6 |
| Brazil | Various |
| China | Various |
| India | Various |
| ... and many more countries |

**Curriculum patterns**:
- American-Patterned Curriculum
- British-Patterned Curriculum
- Caribbean Examinations Council (CXC/CAPE)
- Central Board of Secondary Education (CBSE)
- French-Patterned Education
- International Baccalaureate (IB)
- West Africa Examinations Council (WAEC/WASSCE)

### 2.3 English Language Proficiency Requirements

**Undergraduate requirements** (if English is not first language):

| Test | Minimum Score |
|------|---------------|
| IELTS Academic | 6.5 overall, no band below 6.0 |
| IELTS Online Indicator | 6.5 overall, no band below 6.0 |
| Cambridge B2 First/C1 Advanced/C2 Proficiency | 176 overall, no individual below 169 |
| TOEFL (after Jan 2026) | 4.5 on listening band, no other band below 4.0 |
| TOEFL (Jan 2026 or earlier) | 550 paper-based or 83 IBT, no band below 20 |
| CAEL | 60 overall, no individual below 50 |
| Pearson PTE | 58 or better |
| Duolingo English Test (DET) | 115 or higher, no sub-score below 95 |

**Exemptions**: Completion of 3 years in a Canadian, British or American-patterned secondary school where language of instruction is English.

**Graduate requirements**: Check Graduate Studies Language Requirements page.

### 2.4 IB and AP Recognition

- IB Admission and Recognition available
- AP Admission and Recognition available
- Advanced Credit Equivalency Charts available

## Section 3 — 学费与费用 (Tuition & Fees)

### 3.1 Undergraduate Tuition 2025-2026 (per half-credit course)

| Residency | Arts | Commerce | Science & Engineering | Co-op |
|-----------|------|----------|----------------------|-------|
| Nova Scotia (Canadian/PR) | $790.70 | $916.70 | $891.70 | $500.00 |
| Out of Province (Canadian/PR) | $919.00 | $1,045.00 | $1,020.00 | $500.00 |
| New International Students (2025-26) | $3,001.00 | $3,001.00 | $3,034.00 | $500.00 |

*Note: Tuition includes per-course mandatory fees: Arts Learning Resource ($12), Science Technology ($45), Sobey School of Business Career Services ($12).*
*Nova Scotia residents receive NS University Student Bursary of $128.30 per half-credit course.*
*Undergraduate Costs Calculator available online.*
