## Section 3 — Application Requirements & Deadlines

### 3.1 Undergraduate Entry Requirements

#### Ontario Secondary School Applicants
- OSSD with at least six 4U/4M courses
- Prerequisite courses must be 4U-level
- Minimum averages vary by program (70%–90%+ depending on competitiveness)
- Calculated on best 6 4U/4M courses
- Offers made on a rolling basis

#### International Applicants
- Completion of senior secondary education equivalent to Ontario Grade 12
- Specific country requirements depend on education system
- IB: completion of IB Diploma
- GCE: A-Levels
- AP: Advanced Placement exams
- Transfer credits evaluated upon admission

#### Mature Students
- Applicants 21+ who do not meet standard requirements
- Reviewed on individual basis

#### Special Student Status
- For those not pursuing a degree but wishing to take courses

### 3.2 English Language Requirements

**Undergraduate Programs:**
| Test | Minimum Score | Notes |
|------|-------------|-------|
| **IELTS Academic** | Overall 6.5 | Most common; some programs require higher |
| **TOEFL iBT** | 83 (min 20 Writing) | 220 computer-based test also accepted |
| **ELIP** | 75% in Level 3 | English Language Improvement Program at UWindsor |
| **Pearson PTE** | 60 | Accepted for some programs |

**Graduate Programs (General):**
| Test | Minimum Score |
|------|-------------|
| **IELTS** | 6.5 (varies by program) |
| **TOEFL iBT** | 83–100 (varies; MFA Film requires 100) |
| **Pearson PTE** | 60–68 (varies; MFA Film requires 68) |

**Specific Graduate Program Requirements:**
- MFA Film Production: TOEFL 100; IELTS 7.0; Pearson 68
- Other programs: see individual program profiles

**Exemptions:**
- 3+ years full-time study at an institution where English is the primary language of instruction
- Completion of a degree from an English-speaking institution

### 3.3 Application Deadlines

#### Undergraduate — International Students

| Term | Recommended Deadline | Notes |
|------|-------------------|-------|
| Fall (September) | **May 1** | Applications accepted after deadline if space permits |
| Winter (January) | **September 1** | Programs may fill early |
| Spring (May) | **January 1** | Limited program availability |

#### Undergraduate — Domestic Students

| Term | Deadline |
|------|----------|
| Fall | August 1 |
| Winter | December 1 |
| Spring/Summer | April 1 |

#### Specific Program Deadlines
- Year III Honours Bachelor of Social Work: December 2
- Year III Social Work and Diaspora Studies: December 2
- Year III Social Work and Women's Studies: December 2
- Bachelor of Science in Nursing: March 1

#### Graduate Programs
- Deadlines vary by program (8–12 months before start recommended for international applicants)
- Check individual program profiles for specific dates

### 3.4 How to Apply

#### Undergraduate — International
Three ways to apply:
1. **Apply Directly to UWindsor** via the online application portal
2. **Apply via OUAC** (Ontario Universities' Application Centre) — www.ouac.on.ca
3. **Apply through an Authorized Educational Agent**

#### Undergraduate — Ontario Students
- Apply via OUAC (Ontario Secondary School students use 101 form; all others use 105 form)

#### Undergraduate — Part-time
- Use the University of Windsor part-time application form: www.uwindsor.ca/ptapp

#### Graduate
Two ways to apply:
1. **Apply Directly to UWindsor** via the UWinsite electronic application system
2. **Apply through an Authorized Educational Agent**

### 3.5 Required Documents

#### Undergraduate — International
After applying, submit via UWinsite Student:
- Official senior secondary education diploma
- Official senior secondary education transcripts
- Official post-secondary education diploma (if applicable)
- Official post-secondary education transcripts (if applicable)
- Proof of English language proficiency
- Portfolio (if applicable — e.g., Acting, Film, Visual Arts)
- Course descriptions (for credit transfer)

*Note: Translations required if documents not in English. Use official translation services.*

#### Graduate
- Transcripts of all university/college-level work
- Letters of recommendation (typically 3)
- Statement of interest/purpose
- CV/Resume
- Proof of English proficiency
- Portfolio/writing samples (varies by program)

### 3.6 Application Processing
- **Undergraduate**: Application acknowledgement email within 5 business days; evaluation takes up to 4 weeks after all documents received
- **Graduate**: Application acknowledgement within 2 business days; timelines vary by program
- Monitor status via UWinsite Student

## Section 4 — Costs & Financial Aid

### 4.1 Tuition Fee Structure

- Tuition fees set by the Board of Governors and subject to change without notice
- Fee structure varies by: student category (Ontario resident, Non-Ontario Canadian, International), level (UG/Grad), program/faculty, course load
- **Fee Estimator**: https://my.uwindsor.ca/fee-estimator/ (requires login)
- Fees posted for upcoming academic year in late May

### 4.2 International Tuition (Estimates)

| Level | Estimated Annual Tuition (CAD) |
|-------|-------------------------------|
| Undergraduate (Arts, Science) | ~$28,000–$35,000 |
| Undergraduate (Engineering) | ~$35,000–$42,000 |
| Undergraduate (Business) | ~$32,000–$38,000 |
| Graduate (MA/MSc) | ~$22,000–$30,000 |
| Graduate (MBA) | ~$35,000–$45,000 |
| Graduate (MEng) | ~$28,000–$35,000 |

*Note: Exact fees vary by program. Contact the Cashier's Office for current fee schedules.*

### 4.3 Scholarships & Financial Aid

| Scholarship/Aid | Value | Eligibility |
|-----------------|-------|-------------|
| **Entrance Scholarships** | Varies ($1,000–$10,000+) | Based on admission average |
| **International Student Scholarships** | Varies | International students with strong academics |
| **In-Course Scholarships** | Varies | Current students with high GPA |
| **Graduate Assistantships** | Tuition + stipend | Thesis-based graduate students |
| **OSAP** | Need-based | Ontario residents |
| **US Federal Student Aid** | Varies | US citizens |
| **Ignite (Work Study)** | Part-time wages | Financial need |

- UWindsor awards ~$14 million in scholarships annually
- **Student Awards and Financial Aid**: https://www.uwindsor.ca/studentawards/

### 4.4 Other Costs

| Cost Item | Notes |
|-----------|-------|
| Application fees | Via OUAC or direct; non-refundable |
| Ancillary fees | Mandatory student fees |
| Residence + meal plan | Guaranteed for first-year if applied early |
| UHIP (health insurance) | Mandatory for international students |
| Study permit costs | For international applicants |
| Service fees | $10–$50 for various services |

**Free Tuition**: Students 65 years and over may qualify for free tuition (Canadian citizens/PR only, excluding professional programs).

### 4.5 Fee Payment & Refunds

- Fees due before start of regular term classes
- Payment via UWinsite Student
- **Refund schedule for 12-week term**: Full refund during weeks 1–4; full fees payable after week 4
- Non-payment may result in registration cancellation ($50 reinstatement fee)
- Interest charged daily on outstanding accounts
