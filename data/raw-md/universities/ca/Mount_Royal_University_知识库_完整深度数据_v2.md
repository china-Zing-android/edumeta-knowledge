## Section 3 — Application Requirements & Deadlines

### 3.1 Applicant Categories

| 申请人类型 | 定义 |
|-----------|------|
| High School Applicant | Current high school students or graduates who have not attended any post-secondary institution |
| Post-Secondary Applicant | Students who have attended any post-secondary institution after high school |
| Mature Applicant | 21+ years of age, out of high school for at least one year, and has not attended post-secondary |

### 3.2 Competitive Admission

Admission to Mount Royal University is **competitive**. Students with higher grades are considered first. Grade requirements vary by program. The Competitive Average Range for each program is based on the previous year's admission cycle and serves as a guideline. Some programs require additional criteria such as an interview or portfolio.

**General admission requirements by category:**
- **High School**: Five Grade 12 level courses, including required subjects for the intended program. Mix of Group A, B, C courses.
- **Post-Secondary**: Based on post-secondary coursework.
- **Mature**: Based on mature applicant criteria.

### 3.3 English Language Proficiency

All applicants whose first language is not English must demonstrate English Language Proficiency. Specific score requirements (IELTS/TOEFL/PTE/Duolingo) are published on the university's ELP page. *Note: The ELP page was temporarily inaccessible due to an ongoing cyber incident at the time of data collection.*

## Section 4 — Costs & Financial Aid

### 4.1 Application & Deposits

| 费用类型 | 金额 | 说明 |
|---------|------|------|
| Application Fee | $100 (non-refundable) | Must accompany every admission/readmission application |
| Tuition Deposit (New Students) | $425 (non-refundable) | Confirms acceptance of offer; credited toward tuition |
| Registration Deposit (Continuing Students) | $125 (non-refundable per semester) | Confirms intent to register; credited toward tuition |

### 4.2 Tuition Fee Schedules

Tuition fee schedules are published online but the detailed rate tables (Domestic Fall/Winter, International Fall/Winter, etc.) were **temporarily inaccessible** at the time of data collection due to an ongoing cyber incident. Key known policies:

- **Domestic (Alberta) tuition**: Per-credit fee model, published annually by the Office of the Registrar
- **International student tuition**: Higher per-credit rate; international students are defined as those without Canadian Citizenship or Permanent Resident status
- **Special program fees**: Aviation (flight training fees), Interior Design, Broadcast Media Studies, and Social Work have unique tuition structures due to delivery costs
- **Technology Enhanced Resource Fees**: Maximum $125 per 3-credit course
- **Re-registration fee**: $150 (non-refundable) if registration is cancelled due to non-payment
- **Unclassified Registration Fee**: $85 (non-refundable)
