## Section 3 — Application requirements & deadlines

### 3.1 Entry requirements (Undergraduate)

#### High School Applicants

**Academic requirements:**
- Graduation from high school with minimum 67.0% (C+ / 2.50 GPA) average
- Minimum 60.0% (C grade) in English Studies / English Language Arts
- 5 Grade 12 academic/elective courses (varies by province)
- BC/Yukon: English Studies 12 or English First Peoples 12 + two Grade 12 academic courses + two additional Grade 12 courses
- Ontario: English 12U (ENG4U) + two 12U/12M academic + three additional 12U/12M courses
- Quebec: Minimum one year full-time CEGEP including English 603/604 with min grade C
- International Baccalaureate: Minimum 24 points recommended; IB English A with grade 5+
- SAT/ACT: NOT required for US applicants

**Portfolio requirements:**
- 10–20 images OR 10 min video/audio OR combination
- Process documentation (PDF up to 10 pages or video up to 3 min)
- Studio Assignment (respond to a prompt)
- Writing Assignment (100–250 words)

#### Post-Secondary Transfer Applicants

- Minimum CGPA 2.0 on all attempted undergraduate studies
- 15+ credits completed for transfer consideration
- Portfolio may be waived for BC transfer applicants to BFA Visual Arts (CGPA 3.0+)

#### Mature Students (age 21+)

- C+ (67%) average between Grade 12 English + two Grade 12 academic courses
- Must submit portfolio
- Contact admissions to request mature student consideration

#### Indigenous Learner Pathway (age 19+)

- Two letters of reference
- Personal statement
- Portfolio
- Reviewed by Aboriginal Admissions Committee

### 3.2 English Language Proficiency (ELP)

**Accepted tests and minimum scores:**

| Test | Minimum Score |
|------|---------------|
| IELTS Academic | Overall 6.5 (min 6.0 each component) |
| TOEFL iBT (before Jan 21, 2026) | 84 total (min 20 speaking/reading/listening, 18 writing) |
| TOEFL (from Jan 21, 2026) | Overall 4.5 (min 4.0 each skill) |
| PTE Academic | 56 |
| Duolingo English Test (DET) | 110 |
| CAEL | Overall 70 |
| Cambridge CAE | Scale score 176 (min 169 each) |
| Cambridge CPE | Scale score 176 (min 169 each) |
| CELPIP | Academic Level 4L |
| MELAB | 81 |

**Exemptions:**
- Canadian high school: 3 years consecutive full-time English education (min 60%)
- International high school: 4 years from qualifying country
- IB: IB English A with grade 5+
- AP: AP English Language/Composition or Literature with grade 4+
- Post-secondary: 6 credits of English from recognized institution

**Creative Art Pathway (CAP):** For international students who meet academic/portfolio requirements but need additional English preparation (offered with Vancouver Community College).
