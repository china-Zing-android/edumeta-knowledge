### 0.5 结构性规则 (Rule 5 — structural rules)

1. **Foundation Year availability**: Most BSc/BA programmes offer a 4-year variant with Foundation Year for students not meeting standard entry requirements
2. **Integrated Masters**: Science subjects (Biology, Computer Science, Engineering, Environmental Science, Geography, Marine Biology, Ocean Sciences, Zoology) offer 4-year integrated masters (MBiol, MComp, MEng, MEnvSci, MGeog, MSci, MZool)
3. **Welsh medium options**: Several programmes available in Welsh medium (marked with "Welsh medium" or "Cymraeg")
4. **UCAS codes**: All UG courses have unique UCAS codes; codes starting with same letter indicate related subjects
5. **Duration**: Standard UG is 3 years (4 with Foundation Year); Medicine is 5 years (6 for Gateway); Modern Languages include year abroad (4 years)

## SECTION 3 — Application requirements & deadlines

### 3.1 Entry Requirements

**Standard UG Entry Requirements:**
- Offers are tariff based: 112-136 tariff points from Level 3 qualifications
- A Levels: General Studies and Key Skills not normally accepted
- BTEC National Extended Diploma: DMM - DDD
- Cambridge Technical Extended Diploma: DMM - DDD
- International Baccalaureate Diploma: accepted
- Access: pass required
