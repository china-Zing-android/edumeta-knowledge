### 0.2 学院 / 系层级结构 (Rule 2 — hierarchy)

DMU 由三个学院组成（2025年9月重组）：

#### 学院1: Technology, Arts, and Culture (TAC)
- **创建时间**: 2025年9月
- **合并自**: Faculty of Art, Design and Humanities + Faculty of Computing, Engineering and Media
- **下属学校/系所**:
  - School of Engineering
  - Leicester Media School
  - School of Computing
  - School of Architecture
  - School of Speech and Drama
- **学科领域**: Architecture, Fine Art, Contour Fashion（欧洲唯一）, Footwear Design（英国唯一）, Knitwear Technology, English, History, Computing, Engineering, Media

#### 学院2: Faculty of Business and Law
- **下属学校/系所**:
  - Leicester Castle Business School
  - Leicester De Montfort Law School
  - School of Accounting, Finance and Economics
- **学科领域**: Accounting, Finance, Business, Marketing, Law, Economics, Management

#### 学院3: Faculty of Health and Life Sciences
- **规模**: DMU最大学院，约500名员工，8,000名学生
- **下属学校/系所**:
  - Leicester School of Allied Health Sciences
  - School of Applied Social Sciences
  - Leicester School of Nursing and Midwifery
  - Leicester School of Pharmacy
- **学科领域**: Biomedical Science, Medical Science, Forensic Science, Paramedic Science, Pharmacy, Pharmaceutical Sciences, Psychology, Social Work, Nursing (Adult, Child, Mental Health, Learning Disability), Midwifery, Speech and Language Therapy, Counselling and Psychotherapy, Criminology, Policing Practice

## SECTION 3 — Application requirements & deadlines

### 3.1 英语语言要求 (English Language Requirements)

#### 本科 (Undergraduate)

| 学科领域 | IELTS 要求 |
|---------|-----------|
| Art and Design, Business, Computing, Engineering | 6.0 |
| Law | 6.5 |
| Humanities and Media | 6.0-6.5 |
| Science | 6.5-7.5 |
| MPharm, Nursing, Midwifery, Audiology, Speech & Language Therapy | 7.0 |
| Foundation/International Year Zero | 4.5 (with pre-sessional English) |
| DMU Foundation courses | 6.0 |

**一般要求**: IELTS 6.0 overall with 5.5 in each band (or equivalent)

#### 研究生 (Postgraduate)

| 学科领域 | IELTS 要求 |
|---------|-----------|
| Business and Computing | 6.0 |
| Art, Design and Engineering | 6.0-6.5 |
| Humanities, Media, Law and Science | 6.5 |
| LLM | 7.0 |
| PhD | 6.5 |

#### 其他认可的英语考试
- Integrated Skills in English Trinity College Level 2/3
- WAEC (Grade 6 or above) - 部分国家
- NECO (C6 or above) - 尼日利亚
- KCSE English Language at C or above - 肯尼亚
- Tanzania Secondary School Leaving Certificate or iGCSE (Grade C or above)
- ZIMSEC O-Level English - Grade C or above

#### IELTS 豁免条件
- **约旦**: 在指定公立大学完成100%英语授课学士学位
- **土耳其**: 在指定大学（约47所）完成100%英语授课学士学位
- **泰国**: 从泰国国际课程大学毕业且MOI为英语

### 3.2 学术入学要求 (Academic Entry Requirements)

#### 通用要求

| 要素 | 要求 |
|------|------|
| UCAS 分数范围 | 104-136 |
| IB 分数范围 | 24-32 |
| Foundation 课程 | 55%+ (no module below 50%) |
| American High School Diploma | GPA 3.0+ with ACT 23+ or SAT1 1100/1600 + 2 APs grade 3+ |

#### 中国学生要求

**本科 (Undergraduate)**:
- 高考 (Gaokao) 70% overall average
- A-levels 或认可的 Foundation 课程
- 完成中国大学一年级，70% overall，所有科目 Pass
- DipHE (包括 BTEC 和 SQA HND) 可申请2/3年级入学，75% average 或 GPA 2

**研究生 (Postgraduate)**:
- 4年制学士学位:
  - 2:2 课程: 65% with all Pass (60%)
  - 2:1 课程: 70% with all Pass
- 仅有毕业证书:
  - 2:2 课程: 70% overall, all Pass (60%)
  - 2:1 课程: 75% overall, all Pass (60%)
- 3年制大专:
  - 2:2 课程: 70% overall + 相关工作经验
  - 2:1 课程: 75% + 相关工作经验
- Pre-master's:
  - UK pre-master's: 55% overall, all Pass (40%)
  - Chinese pre-master's: 70% overall, all Pass (60%)

#### 印度学生要求

**本科**: Standard 12 四门最佳科目（不含 Hindi）平均 65%
**研究生**: Bachelor's degree 50-60%（取决于大学和课程）

#### 美国学生要求

**本科**: High School Diploma GPA 3.0+ + 2 AP subjects grade 3+
**研究生**: 
- 2:2 课程: GPA 2.8
- 2:1 课程: GPA 3.2

### 3.3 申请流程

1. 通过 UCAS 申请（UCAS code: D26）
2. 注册 UCAS Hub 账户
3. 填写 UCAS 申请表
4. 支付 UCAS 费用
5. 在截止日期前提交

### 3.4 申请截止日期

- UCAS 主要截止日期: 1月25日（通常）
- 部分课程有不同截止日期
- 国际学生建议提前申请以留出签证处理时间
