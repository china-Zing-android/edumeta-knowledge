## Section 0 — University Overview

The University of Northampton (UON) is a public university in Northampton, England. Established in 2005 (gaining university status), it operates from a single campus at Waterside. UON is a TEF Silver award holder, Living Wage accredited, and ranked in The Times/Sunday Times Good University Guide.

**Campus:** Waterside Campus, University Drive, Northampton NN1 5PH
**Website:** https://www.northampton.ac.uk/
**Phone (UK):** 0300 303 2772 | **International:** 01604 892134
**Founded:** 2005 (university status; predecessor institutions date to 1899)
**Student population:** ~14,000

- Apply via **UCAS** (Universities and Colleges Admissions Service)

- Equal consideration deadline: **31 January** (main cycle); late applications accepted through Clearing
- Many UG programmes list UCAS Tariff points or specific A-Level requirements on individual course pages
