## Institution at a glance

- **Name**: Northumbria University at Newcastle (Northumbria University)
- **Location**: Newcastle upon Tyne, Tyne and Wear, England NE1 8ST, United Kingdom
- **Founded**: 1878 (as a Rutherford Memorial College) / 1969 (as university) / 1992 (university status)
- **Motto**: "The University of Discovery"
- **Modern University of the Year 2025** (The Times and Sunday Times Good University Guide 2025)
- **Top 25 for Research Power** in the UK (Research Excellence Framework, REF 2021)
- **Gold for Student Outcomes and Silver overall** (Teaching Excellence Framework, TEF 2023)
- **Total students**: 34,500+ (UG + PG), drawn from 140+ countries
- **School structure**: 3 Faculties, 12 Schools, ~320 courses/programs

## Section 3 — Application requirements & deadlines

### 3.1 Undergraduate (Home / UK)

- **Entry requirements**: Typical offers are in the range of **120–152 UCAS Tariff points** (A-level BBB–AAA or BTEC DDM–DDD equivalents), varying by program.
- **GCSE**: English Language and Mathematics at grade C/4 or above (or recognised equivalent).
- **Applications via**: UCAS (primary route) or direct application for some programs.
- **Application deadlines**: UCAS main scheme deadline ~January (most courses); Clearing opens early July and runs through September.
- **Source**: <https://www.northumbria.ac.uk/study-at-northumbria/coming-to-northumbria/>

### 3.2 International (non-UK) applicants

- **Country-specific entry requirements**: Listed on Northumbria and Your Country pages
  <https://www.northumbria.ac.uk/international/northumbria-and-your-country/>
- **English language requirements** (general):
  - **Undergraduate**: GCSE English Language or recognised equivalent
  - **Postgraduate**: GCSE English Language, recognised equivalent, OR 2+ years at degree/professional level study in English from an English-speaking country
  - Specific tests accepted: **IELTS Academic, TOEFL iBT (test centre only), PTE Academic (test centre only), LanguageCert Academic, Trinity College ISE, PSI Skills for English, Duolingo, Cambridge Advanced English, INTO English, Pearson Password, Kaplan KIC/KEELT, QAHE English Language Test, International Baccalaureate (Literature/Language and Literature), European Baccalaureate, GCSE/International GCSE English**
  - Source: <https://www.northumbria.ac.uk/international/english-language-centre/acceptable-english-qualifications/>
- **Application process**: Direct application via Northumbria website OR UCAS (UG). Use of official Education Agents permitted; Global Offices offer free 1:1 support.
- **Application guide**: <https://www.northumbria.ac.uk/international/international-application-guide/>
- **Key dates & deadlines**: <https://www.northumbria.ac.uk/study-at-northumbria/admissions/application-deadlines-and-key-dates/>

### 3.3 Postgraduate

- **Entry requirements**: 2:2 (or 2:1 for some programs) honours degree in a relevant discipline; specific programs (e.g. health professional) require professional registration.
- **English language**: As above (postgraduate requirement).
- **Applications**: Direct via Northumbria website; no UCAS for PG.

### 3.4 Portfolios / Interviews

- **Portfolios** required for design, fine art, architecture programs: <https://www.northumbria.ac.uk/study-at-northumbria/coming-to-northumbria/portfolios/>
- **Interviews** required for some health, education, psychology programs: <https://www.northumbria.ac.uk/study-at-northumbria/admissions/interviews/>
