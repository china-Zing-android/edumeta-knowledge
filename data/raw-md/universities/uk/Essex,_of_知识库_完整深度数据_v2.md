## SECTION 3 — Application Requirements & Deadlines

### 3.1 Entry Requirements (UG)

| Requirement Type | Typical Range |
|------------------|---------------|
| **A-Level** | BBC - ABB (112-128 UCAS tariff points) |
| **IB Diploma** | 28-32 points |
| **BTEC** | DMM - DDM |
| **Foundation Year** | Available for students who don't meet direct entry |
| **Clearing** | CCD - CDD (80-88 UCAS tariff points) |

**Subject-Specific Requirements**:
- **Accounting/Finance**: GCSE Mathematics grade 4/C or above
- **Engineering**: A-Level Mathematics and Physics preferred
- **Computer Science**: A-Level Mathematics preferred
- **Sciences**: A-Level Biology/Chemistry/Physics as appropriate
- **Law**: No specific A-Level subjects required
- **Psychology**: GCSE Mathematics and English grade 4/C or above

### 3.2 English Language Requirements

| Test | UG Requirement | PGT Requirement |
|------|----------------|-----------------|
| **IELTS Academic** | 6.0 overall (min 5.5 in each component) | 6.5 overall (min 5.5 in each component) |
| **TOEFL iBT** | 76 overall (min 17 in Listening, 18 in Reading, 20 in Speaking, 17 in Writing) | 88 overall (min 17 in Listening, 18 in Reading, 20 in Speaking, 17 in Writing) |
| **PTE Academic** | 50 overall (min 42 in each component) | 58 overall (min 42 in each component) |
| **Cambridge English** | 169 overall (min 162 in each component) | 176 overall (min 162 in each component) |

**Exemptions**:
- Native English speakers
- Students who have completed a degree in English
- Students from majority English-speaking countries

### 3.3 Application Deadlines

| Application Type | Deadline |
|------------------|----------|
| **UCAS (UG)** | 31 January (main deadline) |
| **UCAS (UG) Late** | 30 June |
| **UCAS (UG) Clearing** | September |
| **PGT (International)** | 31 July |
| **PGT (Home)** | 31 August |
| **PGR** | Rolling (apply anytime) |

### 3.4 Professional Accreditations

- **Accounting**: ACCA, CIMA, ICAEW

- **Psychology**: BPS (British Psychological Society)
