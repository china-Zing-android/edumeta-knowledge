### 1.2 入学要求示例 (Entry requirements example — Computer Science BSc)

**A-Levels**: AAA including Maths, or AAB including Maths and Computer Science

**IB**: 37 points (6,6,6 at HL) including HL Maths, or 35 points (6,6,5 at HL) including HL Maths and Computer Science

**BTEC**: Extended National Diploma D*DD plus A-level Maths at Grade A

**GCSE**: English Language grade 4/C

**Contextual Offer**: A-level ABB including Maths or BBB including Maths and Computer Science

**UCAS Code**: G400 (3 years) / G401 (4 years with placement)

**Duration**: 3 years (or 4 years with placement year)

**Professional Accreditation**: British Computer Society (BCS)

## SECTION 3 — Application requirements & deadlines

### 3.1 英语语言要求 (English language requirements)

**标准要求**: IELTS 6.5 overall, with 6.0 in each individual element (reading, writing, listening and speaking)

**注意事项**:
- 部分学院/系所可能要求更高的英语水平
- 英语语言测试必须在课程开始前两年内完成
- 未达到 IELTS 要求的学生可申请学前英语课程 (pre-sessional English language programmes)

**接受的英语资格**:
- IELTS (Academic/UKVI)
- TOEFL iBT
- Pearson Test of English (PTE) Academic
- Cambridge English Qualifications
- 其他 UKVI 认可的英语测试

详见: https://www.lboro.ac.uk/international/apply/english-language-requirements/

### 3.2 UCAS 申请 (UG application)

**申请方式**: 通过 UCAS 系统申请

**UCAS 代码**: 各课程不同（例如 Computer Science BSc: G400/G401）

**申请截止日期**: 通常为 1 月 25 日（UCAS 主要截止日期）
