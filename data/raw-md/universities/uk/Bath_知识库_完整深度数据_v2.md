### 0.5 结构规则 (Rule 5 — structural rules)

1. **UG courses come in variants**: Each programme typically offers 3-year BSc/BEng, 4-year integrated master's (MEng/MComp/MSci/MMath), and options with professional placement or study abroad (extending duration by 1 year)
2. **27 subject areas group all 200 UG courses**: Subject areas map to departments/faculties
3. **PG courses offer full-time and part-time variants**: Most MSc courses have both 1-year full-time and 2-year part-time options
4. **Professional placement is a key feature**: Most courses offer a placement year option, with reduced fees during placement
5. **Band-based fee structure for international students**: Three bands (Humanities, Economics/Management, Science/Engineering) determine overseas fees

**A-Level**: A\*A\*A including A in Mathematics
**Contextual A-Level**: A\*AA or A\*A\*B including A in Mathematics
**IB**: 36 points overall with 7, 7, 6 in three HL subjects including 6 in HL Mathematics
**Contextual IB**: 36 points with 7,6,6 or 7,7,5 at HL including 6 in HL Mathematics
**GCSE**: English Language/Literature grade 4/C minimum; typically expect grade 8-9 in Mathematics or Physics

**Other accepted qualifications**:
- BTEC Extended Diploma: D\*D\*D plus A in A-Level Mathematics
- T Level: D\* overall (A\* in core) plus A in A-Level Mathematics
- Scottish Highers: AA in two Advanced Highers including Mathematics
- Access to HE Diploma: 42 Distinction credits including 12 in Mathematics
- US Advanced Placement: 5,5,5,5 in four APs including Calculus BC
