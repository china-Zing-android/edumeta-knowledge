### 1.2 UG 典型入学要求（以 BSc Computer Science 为例）

| 考试类型 | 要求 |
|----------|------|
| A-level | CCC |
| BTEC | DMM |
| IB | 27 points overall, 三门 HL 555 |
| Access | Pass with 45 Level 3 credits including 24 Distinctions |
| Scottish Higher | CCCCCD (Higher) 或 DDD (Advanced Higher) |
| European Baccalaureate | 65% |
| Irish Leaving Certificate | H3 H3 H3 H3 |
| GCSE Mathematics | 若无理科/数学 A-level，需 Grade B/Grade 6 |
