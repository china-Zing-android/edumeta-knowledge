#### Contextual Offers
- Usually **one or two grades lower** than standard entry requirements
- Eligibility based on postcode (POLAR4 quintile 1-2), school performance, or care experience
- Example: Standard ABB → Contextual BBB or BBC

#### EPQ (Extended Project Qualification)
- Accepted as part of offers
- Example: Computer Science — BBB + EPQ at grade B

#### Other Accepted Qualifications
- **AS-Levels**: Two AS-levels can replace one A-Level
- **Access to HE Diploma**: Pass with 45 credits at Level 3, 30 at Distinction
- **T Levels**: Accepted (e.g., Distinction in Digital Production for CS)
- **Scottish Highers**: Refer to individual course pages
- **Irish Leaving Certificate**: Refer to individual course pages

### 3.2 Application Process

#### Undergraduate
- Apply through **UCAS** (Universities and Colleges Admissions Service)
- UCAS code: **L34** (University of Leicester)
- Maximum 5 choices
- Personal statement required
- Reference required

#### Postgraduate
- Apply directly through the university website
- Supporting documents: transcripts, personal statement, references
- Some courses may require portfolio or interview

### 3.3 Deposit Requirements (International Students)
- Required for students resident outside UK and Republic of Ireland
- Subtracted from total tuition fee
- Must be paid to secure place after offer acceptance
