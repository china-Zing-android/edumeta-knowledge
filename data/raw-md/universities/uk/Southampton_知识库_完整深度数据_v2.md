### 1.2 Foundation Year programmes

Southampton offers foundation year routes for students who don't meet direct entry requirements. Foundation year fees are £26,000 for international students. Available in:

- Engineering (all disciplines)
- Computer Science
- Sciences (Biology, Chemistry, Physics, Mathematics)
- Geology
- Marine Biology

### 1.3 Year Abroad / Placement Year variants

Many programmes offer extended variants:
- **Year in Industry/Placement Year**: Fees at 20% of standard annual fee during placement year
- **Year Abroad**: Fees at 15% of standard annual fee during abroad year
- **Study Abroad @ Southampton**: £24,800 per year

## SECTION 3 — Application requirements & deadlines

### 3.1 Undergraduate application process

**Application system**: UCAS (Universities and Colleges Admissions Service)
**UCAS Institution Code**: S27

**Key UCAS deadlines (2026 entry):**
- **15 October 2025**: Medicine, Dentistry, Veterinary Science, and all courses at Oxford and Cambridge
- **29 January 2026**: All other undergraduate courses (equal consideration deadline)
- **30 June 2026**: Late applications (clearing opens 5 July 2026)

**Term dates 2026 entry:**
- Welcome Weekend: 12 September 2026
- Freshers' Week: 14 September 2026
- Autumn Term: 21 September - 12 December 2026

### 3.2 General entry requirements

**GCSE requirements (all programmes):**
- English Language (or English): minimum grade 4/C
- Mathematics: minimum grade 4/C
- Medicine requires 7 GCSEs at grade B/6 or above including English, Maths, and Sciences

**A-Level typical offers by tier:**
- **A*AA**: Engineering (MEng), Computer Science (MEng), Medicine, competitive sciences
- **AAA**: Most Engineering (BEng), Sciences, Computer Science (BSc), Law, Psychology
- **AAB**: Business, Economics, Social Sciences, Nursing, Education
- **ABB**: Arts, Humanities, English, History, Archaeology
- **BBB**: Contextual offers (reduced by 1-2 grades for eligible UK students)

**IB typical offers:**
- **38 pts (19 at HL)**: Engineering MEng, competitive sciences
- **36 pts (18 at HL)**: Most AAA programmes
- **34 pts (16 at HL)**: AAB programmes
- **32 pts (16 at HL)**: ABB programmes

### 3.3 English language requirements

Southampton uses a **band system (A through I)** for English language requirements. Based on extracted course data:

**Most common requirement (Band equivalent):**
| Test | Overall | Listening | Reading | Writing | Speaking |
|------|---------|-----------|---------|---------|----------|
| IELTS Academic | 6.5 | 6.0 | 6.0 | 6.0 | 6.0 |

| Test | Overall | Listening | Reading | Writing | Speaking |
|------|---------|-----------|---------|---------|----------|
| IELTS Academic | 7.0 | 7.0 | 7.0 | 7.0 | 7.0 |

- IELTS Academic UKVI SELT
- LanguageCert Academic UKVI SELT

*Online Tests:*

**Alternative qualifications accepted:**
- GCSE English Language grade 4/C (covers bands A-I)
- IB English A (SL or HL): Grade 4
- IB English B: SL grade 6 or HL grade 4
- Indian Standard XII (CBSE/ISC): 70%
- Hong Kong HKDSE English: Grade 4
- Malaysian SPM (2021+): CEFR B2 overall

**Test validity**: Must be taken within 2 years of course start date

### 3.4 Postgraduate entry requirements

**Typical requirements by programme level:**
- **MSc/MA**: 2:1 (Upper Second-Class Honours) degree or international equivalent
- **Some programmes**: 2:2 (Lower Second-Class Honours) accepted
- **MBA**: 2:1 degree + minimum 3 years work experience
- **PhD**: Master's degree or strong Bachelor's with research experience

**English language for PGT:**
- Most programmes: IELTS 6.5 (6.0 in all components)
- Some programmes: IELTS 7.0 (6.5 in all components)

### 3.5 Contextual admissions

Southampton offers **reduced offers** for UK students meeting contextual criteria:
- Typically 1-2 grades lower than standard offers
- Based on school performance, postcode, care experience, estrangement, refugee status, military family
