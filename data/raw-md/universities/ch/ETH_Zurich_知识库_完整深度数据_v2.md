### 2.2 At least one program's full deep-dive (worked example)

**Master in Computer Science (D-INFK)**  
- Programme URL: https://ethz.ch/en/studies/master/degree-programmes/engineering-sciences/computer-science.html  
- Degree / credits / duration / language from source listing: “MSc | 120 ECTS | 4 semesters | English | 5 specialisations.”  
- Application model: centralized ETH Master application via eApply; international bachelor applicants use the 1–30 November window.  
- Contact for Master admissions: Admissions Office Master, `master@ethz.ch`, +41 44 632 81 00.  
- English/German proof: per programme; recognized certificate must be submitted by application deadline.  
- GRE/GMAT: ETH global Master application page says standardized tests like GRE are evaluated “if required”; per-programme requirement profile must be checked for this programme.

### 2.3 Graduate admissions model

ETH Zurich Master admissions are centralized at institution level, with programme-specific requirement profiles and department admission committees. Doctoral admission is decentralized: applicants must first find a professor offering a doctoral position; only then can they register for a doctorate.
