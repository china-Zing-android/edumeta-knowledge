## SECTION 1 — Undergraduate

### 1.1 Architecture

NSU is a private R1-Carrigan university (Carnegie: R1-Doc/Very-High, founded 1964). Located in **Davie/Fort Lauderdale, FL**. Diverse portfolio of UG majors.

### 1.2 Undergraduate majors

**Halmos College of Arts and Sciences**: BA/BS in many fields (Biology, Chemistry, Computer Science, Mathematics, Psychology, English, History, Political Science, Sociology, Communication, etc.)

**H. Wayne Huizenga Business**: BSBA, BBA, BS in Accounting / Business Admin / Entrepreneurship / Finance / Marketing / Management / Sport & Recreation Management / Tourism & Hospitality

**Ron and Kathy Assaf Nursing**: BSN generic, Accelerated BSN, RN-BSN

**Computing, AI, Cybersecurity**: BS Computer Science, BS Cybersecurity, BS Information Tech

**Abraham S. Fischler Education**: BS in Education (multiple concentrations), BS in Criminal Justice
