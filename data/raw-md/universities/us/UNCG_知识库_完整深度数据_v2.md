## SECTION 1 — Undergraduate education

### 1.1 Architecture
UNCG is a public R2 research university in Greensboro, NC. Part of the UNC system. Founded 1891 (as a women's college). ~20,000 students. Known for visual/performing arts, education, business, health sciences, and Bryan School of Business.

### 1.2 Undergraduate majors

(Detailed list in Section 0.2 — ~85 majors across 8 schools)

### 1.3 Minors
65+ minors (across most departments)

### 1.4 General Education Requirements
UNCG has a common core: Composition, Mathematics, Science, Social Science, Humanities, Fine Arts, Health/PE, plus World Languages and Cultural Diversity.

## SECTION 3 — Application requirements & deadlines

### 3.1 Undergraduate

| 项 | 要求 |
|---|---|
| Application | Common App or Coalition App |
| Application fee | $60 (waiver available) |
| SAT/ACT | Test-optional (UNC system policy) |
| HS GPA | 2.5+ minimum, 3.0+ recommended |
| English (international) | TOEFL 79 iBT / IELTS 6.5 / PTE 53 |
| Early Action deadline | November 1 (binding for some programs) |
| Regular Decision deadline | January 15 |

### 3.2 Graduate

| 项 | 要求 |
|---|---|
| Application | UNCG Online Application |
| Application fee | $65 |
| English (international) | TOEFL 79 iBT / IELTS 6.5 |
| GRE/GMAT | Varies by program |
| Fall priority deadline | April 1 |
