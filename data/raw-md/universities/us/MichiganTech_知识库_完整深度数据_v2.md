## SECTION 1 — Undergraduate education

### 1.1 Architecture
Michigan Tech is a public R2 research university in Houghton, MI (Upper Peninsula). Founded 1885 as the Michigan Mining School. ~7,000 students. Strong in engineering, sciences, computing, forestry, business. Reorganized into 4 colleges + Graduate School.

### 1.2 Undergraduate majors (grouped by 学院 > 系 > 学位级别)

#### College of Engineering
- Department of Biomedical Engineering: BS Biomedical Engineering
- Department of Chemical Engineering: BS Chemical Engineering
- Department of Civil, Environmental, and Geospatial Engineering: BS Civil Engineering, BS Environmental Engineering, BS Geospatial Engineering, BS Geomatics
- Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering: BS Computer Engineering, BS Electrical Engineering
- Department of Engineering Fundamentals: Engineering (first-year program)
- Department of Geological and Mining Engineering and Sciences: BS Geological Engineering, BS Mining Engineering, BS Applied Geophysics, BS Mining Engineering Technology
- Department of Materials Science and Engineering: BS Materials Science and Engineering, BS Metallurgical Engineering
- Department of Mechanical Engineering — Engineering Mechanics: BS Mechanical Engineering, BS Engineering Mechanics
- Department of Manufacturing and Mechanical Engineering Technology: BS Mechanical Engineering Technology
- School of Business and Economics: BS Accounting, BS Business Analytics, BS Construction Management, BS Economics, BS Engineering Management, BS Finance, BS Management, BS Management Information Systems, BS Marketing, BS Operations & Supply Chain Management

#### College of Computing
- Department of Applied Computing: BS Computer Science, BS Cybersecurity, BS Data Science, BS Software Engineering, BS Computer Network and System Administration
- School of Business (cross-listed): see above

#### College of Forest Resources and Environmental Science
- BS Applied Ecology and Environmental Science
- BS Forest Business Management
- BS Forest Management
- BS Forestry
- BS Wildlife Ecology and Management
- BS Natural Resources Management
- BS Sustainable Bioproducts
- BS Water Science

#### College of Sciences and Arts
- Department of Biological Sciences: BS Biological Sciences, BS Ecology, BS Biochemistry, BS Molecular Biology
- Department of Chemistry: BS Chemistry, BS Medicinal Chemistry
- Department of Physics: BS Physics, BS Applied Physics
- Department of Mathematical Sciences: BS Mathematics, BS Actuarial Science, BS Statistics
- Department of Social Sciences: BA Anthropology, BA Communication, BA Economics, BA History, BA Philosophy, BA Political Science, BA Psychology, BA Sociology, BA Social Science
- Department of Humanities: BA English, BA Fine Arts, BA Music, BA Theatre, BA World Languages and Cultures, BA Liberal Arts
- Department of Visual and Performing Arts: BFA Art and Design

### 1.3 Minors
73 minors (full enumeration requires catalog crawl; some key ones: AI, Astrophysics, Computer Science, Cybersecurity, Data Science, Environmental Studies, GIS, Global Business, Mining Engineering, Robotics, Sustainability, etc.)

### 1.4 General Education Requirements
Michigan Tech Core (general education covering communication, math, science, humanities, social sciences, design). See mtu.edu.

## SECTION 3 — Application requirements & deadlines

### 3.1 Undergraduate (from mtu.edu/admissions)

| 项 | 要求 |
|---|---|
| Application | Michigan Tech Application or Common App |
| Application fee | $0 (free) — very unusual! |
| SAT/ACT | Test-optional (2026 cycle) |
| HS GPA | 2.5+ recommended |
| English (international) | TOEFL 79 iBT / IELTS 6.5 / PTE 53 / Duolingo 100 |
| Fall priority deadline | November 1 (early action) |
| Fall final deadline | Rolling through summer |

### 3.2 Graduate (from mtu.edu/gradschool)

| 项 | 要求 |
|---|---|
| Application | Graduate School Application (online) |
| Application fee | $0 (no fee!) |
| English (international) | TOEFL 79 iBT / IELTS 6.5 / PTE 53 / Duolingo 100 |
| GRE/GMAT | Varies by program |
| Fall priority deadline | Rolling; recommended by April 1 |
