## SECTION 1 — Undergraduate education

### 1.1 Architecture
UMBC is a public R1 research university in Catonsville (suburban Baltimore). Founded 1966. ~14,000 students. Known for sciences, engineering, honors program, and Meyerhoff Scholars (STEM diversity program).

### 1.2 Undergraduate majors (grouped)

#### College of Arts, Humanities and Social Sciences (CAHSS)
- BA in many fields (Anthropology, English, History, Economics, Political Science, Psychology, Sociology, etc.)
- BS in Psychology, Geography, Environmental Science, etc.
- BFA in Visual Arts, Dance
- BSW Social Work

#### College of Engineering and Information Technology (COEIT)
- BS Computer Science
- BS Computer Engineering
- BS Electrical Engineering
- BS Mechanical Engineering
- BS Chemical Engineering
- BS Biochemical Engineering
- BS Environmental Engineering
- BS Information Systems
- BS Business Technology Administration

#### College of Natural and Mathematical Sciences (CNMS)
- BS Biological Sciences
- BS Chemistry
- BS Biochemistry & Molecular Biology
- BS Mathematics
- BS Statistics
- BS Physics
- BS Marine Sciences (joint with IMET)

#### Robert H. Smith School of Business
- BS Accounting
- BS Business Technology Administration
- BS Economics
- BS Finance
- BS Information Systems
- BS Management
- BS Marketing
- BS Operations & Business Analytics

#### Honors College
- Interdisciplinary honors
- Special seminars

### 1.3 Minors
60+ minors (Africana Studies, Animation, Applied Mathematics, Atmospheric Science, Bioinformatics, etc.)

### 1.4 General Education Requirements
UMBC has 4-5 distribution areas: English Composition, Mathematics, Arts & Humanities, Social Sciences, Sciences.

## SECTION 3 — Application requirements & deadlines

### 3.1 Undergraduate

| 项 | 要求 |
|---|---|
| Application | Common App or Coalition App |
| Application fee | $50 (waiver available) |
| SAT/ACT | Test-optional (most programs) |
| HS GPA | 3.0+ recommended |
| English (international) | TOEFL 80 iBT / IELTS 6.5 / PTE 53 / Duolingo 100 |
| Early Action deadline | November 1 |
| Regular Decision deadline | January 15 |
| Honors College deadline | November 1 |

### 3.2 Graduate

| 项 | 要求 |
|---|---|
| Application | UMBC Online Application |
| Application fee | $50-$70 |
| English (international) | TOEFL 80 iBT / IELTS 6.5 |
| GRE/GMAT | Varies (some waived) |
| Fall priority deadline | March 1 |
