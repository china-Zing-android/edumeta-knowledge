## SECTION 1 — Undergraduate education

### 1.1 Architecture
Mississippi State University (MSU) is a public R1 research university in Starkville, MS. Founded 1878. ~22,000 students. Strong in engineering, agriculture, business, education, veterinary medicine.

### 1.2 Undergraduate majors (grouped by 学院 > 系 > 学位级别)

#### College of Agriculture and Life Sciences
- Department of Agricultural Economics: BS Agribusiness, BS Agricultural Economics
- Department of Agricultural and Biological Engineering: BS Agricultural Engineering Technology, BS Biological Engineering
- Department of Animal and Dairy Sciences: BS Animal Science (multiple concentrations)
- Department of Biochemistry, Nutrition and Health Promotion: BS Biochemistry, BS Culinology
- Department of Food Science, Nutrition and Health Promotion: BS Food Science
- Department of Plant and Soil Sciences: BS Agronomy, BS Horticulture, BS Plant Pathology
- School of Human Sciences: BS Human Development and Family Science, BS Agricultural Education

#### College of Architecture, Art and Design
- School of Architecture: Bachelor of Architecture (5-yr), BS Architectural Studies

Many departments offer minors (e.g. Agribusiness, Aerospace Studies, African American Studies, Communication, Computer Science, Criminal Justice, etc.). N/A — full enumeration requires catalog crawl.

MSU has a general education core including English composition, mathematics, natural sciences, humanities, social/behavioral sciences, fine arts. See catalog.msstate.edu.

## SECTION 3 — Application requirements & deadlines

### 3.1 Undergraduate (from admissions.msstate.edu)

| 项 | 要求 |
|---|---|
| Application | MSU Application or Common App |
| Application fee | $40 (waiver available) |
| SAT/ACT | Test-optional (2026 cycle) |
| HS GPA | 2.0+ recommended; competitive 3.0+ |
| English (international) | TOEFL 71 iBT / IELTS 6.0 / PTE 48 / Duolingo 95 |
| Fall priority deadline | April 1 |
| Fall final deadline | Rolling (subject to space) |
| Spring deadline | November 1 |

### 3.2 Graduate (from grad.msstate.edu)

| 项 | 要求 |
|---|---|
| Application | MSU Graduate School Application |
| Application fee | $60 domestic / $80 international |
| English (international) | TOEFL 71 iBT / IELTS 6.0 (some programs require higher) |
| GRE/GMAT | Varies by program |
| Fall priority deadline | April 1 (some programs earlier) |
| Spring priority deadline | November 1 |

## SECTION 4 — Costs & financial aid

### 4.1 Tuition (2025-26, from controller.msstate.edu)

| Tier | Tuition + Fees (annual) |
|------|------------------------|
| MS Resident UG | ~$9,800 |
| Non-resident UG | ~$24,800 |
| MS Resident Grad | ~$10,200 |
| Non-resident Grad | ~$25,200 |

### 4.2 Financial aid
- 90%+ of undergraduates receive some form of aid
- Mississippi residents with Pell eligibility may qualify for state grants
- MSU has named scholarships and departmental awards
