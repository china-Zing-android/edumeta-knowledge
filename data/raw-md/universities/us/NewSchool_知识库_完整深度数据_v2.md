## SECTION 1 — Undergraduate education

### 1.1 Architecture

The New School is a private research university in Greenwich Village, NYC. Founded 1919. Known for design (Parsons), social sciences (NSSR), performing arts (Mannes), and liberal arts (Lang). Strong emphasis on interdisciplinary study.

### 1.2 Undergraduate majors (representative)

| School | Sample Programs |
