## SECTION 1 — Undergraduate education

### 1.1 Architecture
Rutgers University–Camden is a public R2 research university and one of three regional campuses of Rutgers, The State University of New Jersey. Located in Camden, NJ (across from Philadelphia). Founded 1926. ~5,000 students. Smaller than New Brunswick but with its own unique programs.

### 1.2 Undergraduate majors (grouped by 学院 > 系 > 学位级别)

#### Faculty of Arts and Sciences
- Department of Biology: BA Biology, BS Biology
- Department of Chemistry: BA Chemistry, BS Chemistry
- Department of Computer Science: BS Computer Science, BA Computer Science
- Department of English: BA English
- Department of History: BA History
- Department of Mathematics: BS Mathematics
- Department of Physics: BS Physics
- Department of Political Science: BA Political Science
- Department of Psychology: BA Psychology
- Department of Sociology, Anthropology, and Criminal Justice: BA Sociology, BA Anthropology, BA Criminal Justice
- Department of Modern Languages and Literatures: BA Spanish, BA French
- Department of Philosophy and Religion: BA Philosophy, BA Religion
- Department of Economics: BA Economics
- Department of Communication: BA Communication
- Department of Fine Arts: BA Visual Arts, BA Music
- Department of Theater: BFA Theater Arts
- Department of Interdisciplinary Studies: BA Liberal Studies

#### School of Business–Camden
- BS Accounting
- BS Business Administration (multiple concentrations: Finance, Marketing, Management, Supply Chain Management, Entrepreneurship)
- BS Business Analytics

#### School of Nursing–Camden
- BSN (generic BSN, accelerated BSN, RN-BSN)

#### University College–Camden

### 1.3 Minors

### 3.1 Undergraduate (from admissions.camden.rutgers.edu)

|---|---|

### 4.1 Tuition (2025-26)

| NJ Resident UG | ~$16,000 |
| Non-resident UG | ~$33,000 |
