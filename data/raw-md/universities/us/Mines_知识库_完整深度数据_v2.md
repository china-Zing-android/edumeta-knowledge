## SECTION 1 — Undergraduate education

### 1.1 Architecture

Colorado School of Mines is a public R1 research university (Carnegie: R1-Doc/Very-High, founded 1874) located in **Golden, Colorado**. Premier engineering and applied science institution. ~5,500 UG enrollment. **#1 in the world for mineral and mining engineering** (per homepage). **Top 30 Best Value Colleges in America (Niche, 2024)**. **$95M annual research awards** (per homepage, FY2022). **1,300+ companies actively recruit Mines students**.
