## Section 1 — Graduate Education (Taught Master's Programmes)

### 1.1 MBA (Master in Business Administration)

**Degree**: MBA
**Duration**: 10 months (full-time)
**Intakes**: January and August
**Campuses**: France (Fontainebleau) or Singapore (Asia Campus)
**Website**: https://www.insead.edu/master-programmes/master-business-administration

**Class Profile**:
| Attribute | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Class size | 900–950 (across two intakes) |
| Average age | 29 (range 23–35) |
| Work experience | 3–8 years |
| Nationalities | 90 |
| Countries represented | 75 |
| Women | 38% |
| Backgrounds | Consulting, Financial Services, Corporate, Tech/Media/Telcom |

**Curriculum highlights**:
- 75+ diverse electives
- Optional exchange across INSEAD campuses or partner schools (US, China)
- 8-week summer break/internship period

**Tuition & Fees**:
| Item | Amount |
|------|--------|
| Tuition (August 2026 intake) | €109,860 |
| Tuition (January 2027 intake) | €109,860 |
| Business Foundations Programme (in-person, optional) | €2,040 |
| Business Foundations Programme (online, optional) | €1,530 |

Fees include: printed course materials, library access, IT services, student council fees, €150 alumni association subscription, gym, business cards, printing, health insurance.

**Scholarships**:
- 170+ scholarship funds available
- 41% of participants received scholarships
- Average scholarship: €24,000
- Scholarship Finder: https://www.insead.edu/scholarship/listing

**Admissions Criteria**:
1. Ability to Contribute — unique perspectives and experiences
2. Academic Capacity — strong analytical skills
3. International Motivation — curiosity and openness to diverse cultures
4. Leadership Potential — initiative, responsibility, ability to inspire

**Application Requirements**:
- Online application form
- GMAT or GRE scores
- Video interview (Kira platform)
- Job description questions
- Motivation essays
- Language declaration
- Letters of recommendation
- CV/Resume
- Official transcripts
- Campus choice
- Application fee

**Application Deadlines — January 2027 Intake**:
| Round | Application Deadline | Interview Decision | Final Decision |
|-------|--------------------:|------------------:|---------------:|
| Round 1 | 17 March 2026 | 17 April 2026 | 15 May 2026 |
| Round 2 | 21 April 2026 | 29 May 2026 | 26 June 2026 |
| Round 3 | 30 June 2026 | 31 July 2026 | 28 August 2026 |
| Round 4 | 25 August 2026* | 16 September 2026 | 23 October 2026 |

*\*Planning a move into Financial Services or an internship? Apply by 4 August 2026.*

**Admissions Process**:
1. Submit online application (form + documents + video interview)
2. Pre-selection decision (~1 month after deadline)
3. Alumni interviews (two interviews in country of residence)
4. Admissions Committee review
5. Final decision (up to 10 weeks from submission)
