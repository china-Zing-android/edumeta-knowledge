### 0.2 学院 / 系层级结构 (Hierarchy)

SUTD uses a **cluster/pillar-based structure** rather than traditional faculties and departments. There are 7 academic pillars (clusters), of which 5 directly offer undergraduate degree programmes. HASS and SMT provide cross-cutting curriculum support.

```
Singapore University of Technology and Design (SUTD)
├── Architecture and Sustainable Design (ASD)
│   └── Undergraduate: BS in Architecture and Sustainable Design
├── Design and Artificial Intelligence (DAI)
│   └── Undergraduate: BS in Design and Artificial Intelligence
├── Engineering Product Development (EPD)
│   └── Undergraduate: BEng in Engineering Product Development
├── Engineering Systems and Design (ESD)
│   └── Undergraduate: BEng in Engineering Systems and Design
├── Information Systems Technology and Design (ISTD)
│   └── Undergraduate: BEng in Computer Science and Design
├── Humanities, Arts and Social Sciences (HASS) [cross-cutting]
│   └── No standalone degree — provides compulsory HASS education
└── Science, Mathematics and Technology (SMT) [cross-cutting]
    └── No standalone degree — provides foundational STEM education
```

## Section 3 — Application Requirements & Deadlines

### 3.1 Undergraduate Admissions

#### Holistic Admissions Approach

SUTD uses a **comprehensive application review** considering three dimensions:

1. **Academic competency**: Focus on Mathematics and Sciences; final year exam results + academic performance over 2-3 years prior
2. **Qualities/Traits**: Growth mindset, collaboration, curiosity/open-mindedness, drive/self-motivation, resilience, risk-taking
3. **Portfolios**: Co-curricular activities, notable accomplishments, leadership positions, teacher recommendations, personal insight questions
4. **Interview**: Selected candidates attend a conversation with faculty/leadership

#### Entry Requirements by Qualification

##### Singapore-Cambridge GCE A-Level
- **Subject combination**: Good passes in three H2 content-based subjects + General Paper (GP) + Pass grade in Project Work (PW)
- **Recommended**: H2 Mathematics and a H2 Science subject (Physics or Chemistry)
- **MTL requirement**: Singapore Citizens and PRs must fulfil Mother Tongue Language requirement
- **UAS computation** (from AY2026): Three H2 content-based subjects + GP; H1 MTL and/or fourth H1/H2 content-based subject may be considered if it improves UAS
- **Pass in PW required** from AY2026

##### Indicative Grade Profile (IGP) — A-Level students offered in 2025
- ~100% had taken H2 Mathematics (~half scored As or Bs)
- Most had taken H2 Physics or H2 Chemistry (or both), >1/3 scoring As or Bs
- At O-Level, ~100% had scored As or Bs in Mathematics and Sciences

##### Polytechnic Diploma
- Alternative pathway available

##### NUS High School Diploma
- Accepted

##### International Baccalaureate (IB) Diploma
- Accepted (applicants from international schools in Singapore or abroad)

##### International Qualifications (12+ years of education)
- UK, Hong Kong and Brunei A-Level Certificates
- Australian Qualifications
- India Standard 12
- Indonesian Ijazah SMA / Ujian Nasional (UN)
- Malaysia Unified Examination Certificate (UEC)
- Myanmar Matriculation Examination
- Philippines High School Certificate
- PRC National College Entrance Exam (NCEE/Gao Kao)
- Sijil Tinggi Persekolahan Malaysia (STPM)
- Thailand Certificate of Secondary Education (Mathayom 6)
- US High School Diploma
- Vietnam High School Graduation Examination
- Other International Qualifications

#### English Language Proficiency

Compulsory for applicants presenting qualifications where English is **not the medium of instruction** (including Indonesian SMA, Myanmar Matriculation, PRC Gao Kao, UEC, STPM, Vietnam Graduation Exam, etc.):

- **SAT** (SUTD Code: 6532)
- **TOEFL iBT** (SUTD Code: 7216)
- **IELTS**
- **ACT** (SUTD Code: 7989)
- **Pearson Test of English Academic (PTE Academic)**
- **C1 Advanced**

> **Note**: SUTD does not publish specific minimum score thresholds for these tests on its public website. Scores obtained in the two years leading up to application will be considered.
